About

Who I am, and why I'm here.

I'm a senior at Santa Monica High School working through the engineering capstone pathway, with a focus on mechanical engineering. This past summer I attended UCLA COSMOS 2026, and I've written a research paper testing how symbolic regression holds up against noisy data. Outside of school and engineering projects, I work as a lifeguard at Santa Monica College.

This site is my digital engineering notebook for the capstone class — I'll be using it to document my process, share project builds, and post regular blog updates as the year goes on.

Interests

Where mechanical design meets computation.

Mechanical Engineering Prototyping & CAD Applied Math / Symbolic Regression Robotics Data-Driven Design

I'm especially interested in the overlap between mechanical design and computation — using data and modeling to inform physical design decisions, rather than treating them as separate steps.

Goals

What I'm working toward this year.

My goal this year is to take projects from a rough idea through prototyping to a finished build I'm proud to show off — and to get better at documenting that process clearly along the way, not just the final result. I also want to use the WET Invention Challenge and Capstone Project to push into areas of mechanical engineering I haven't had much hands-on experience with yet.
