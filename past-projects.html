What Determines Noise Robustness in Symbolic Regression?

A controlled study testing how well symbolic regression (using PySR) can rediscover physical laws — like Hooke's law or Coulomb's law — from noisy data. Across roughly 6,000 runs on 17 physical laws, the original hypothesis (that recovery depends on how structurally complex an equation is) didn't hold up. Instead, the biggest factor turned out to be whether the equation requires fitting a numeric constant at all — constant-free equations stayed almost perfectly recoverable even at high noise, while constant-bearing ones degraded sharply. Noise tolerance also scaled substantially with how much data was available, meaning a law that looks fragile at a small sample size can be robust at a larger one.
